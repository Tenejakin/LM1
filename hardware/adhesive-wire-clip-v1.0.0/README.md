# LM1 adhesive wire clip v1.0.0

A simple four-channel cable-management clip with a flat back for double-sided
tape. The four snap channels are intended for typical 1.5–3.8 mm electronics
wires or very small wire bundles.

Dimensions are 40 x 12 x 8.5 mm. Each channel is nominally 4.4 mm in diameter
with a 3.0 mm entry. The tape face is a continuous 40 x 12 mm surface.

## Printing

Print the STL flat-side down at 100% scale in millimetres. Use PETG for clips
that flex repeatedly; PLA is suitable if wires will rarely be removed. Suggested
settings are 0.2 mm layers, four walls and 25–40% infill. No supports are needed.

## Installation

Clean the printed mounting surface and the clip with isopropyl alcohol and let
both dry. Cover most of the flat underside with thin, high-bond double-sided
tape, press firmly for 30 seconds, and allow the adhesive to settle before
loading wires. Mount away from hot components and moving parts. Do not route
wires across sharp printed edges or pull the camera ribbon cable tight.

Press one wire at a time through the narrow opening. If the fit is too tight,
scale only the STL's X and Z dimensions to 105%; keep Y at 100% so the adhesive
area and clip length remain unchanged.

The generated STL is validated as one watertight solid. Physical wire fit and
adhesive strength depend on printer calibration, filament and tape.
