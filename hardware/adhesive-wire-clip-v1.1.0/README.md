# LM1 vertical two-wire clip v1.1.0

A compact adhesive-backed clip for two wires running vertically along the
printed side of the LM1 frame. The channels fit typical 1.5–3.8 mm electronics
wires and use the same snap geometry as the four-channel v1.0.0 clip.

The clip is 20 x 16 x 8.5 mm. Mount it with the 16 mm channel direction vertical;
the two wires then run upward and downward through the clip. Its entire flat
20 x 16 mm underside accepts double-sided tape.

Print flat-side down at 100% scale in millimetres. Use 0.2 mm layers, four walls
and 25–40% infill. PETG is recommended for repeated wire removal; PLA is suitable
for a mostly permanent installation. No supports are required.

Clean both mounting surfaces with isopropyl alcohol, allow them to dry, apply
thin high-bond double-sided tape across the flat underside, and press firmly for
30 seconds. Let the adhesive settle before snapping in the wires. Avoid hot
components, moving parts, sharp bends and tension on the camera ribbon cable.

The STL is validated as one watertight solid. Physical fit and adhesive strength
depend on printer calibration, filament and tape.
