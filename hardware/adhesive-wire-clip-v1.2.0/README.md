# LM1 vertical single-wire clip v1.2.0

A compact adhesive-backed clip for one wire running vertically along the
printed LM1 frame. It uses the same snap geometry as the two- and four-channel
clips and fits typical 1.5–3.8 mm electronics wires.

The clip is 10 x 16 x 8.5 mm. Mount it with the 16 mm channel direction vertical.
The complete flat 10 x 16 mm underside accepts double-sided tape.

Print flat-side down at 100% scale in millimetres using 0.2 mm layers, four walls
and 25–40% infill. PETG is recommended for repeated use; PLA is suitable for a
mostly permanent installation. No supports are required.

Clean the clip and mounting surface with isopropyl alcohol and allow both to dry.
Apply thin high-bond double-sided tape across the underside, press firmly for 30
seconds, and let the adhesive settle before inserting the wire. Keep the wire
away from heat, moving parts, sharp edges and tight bends.

The generated STL is validated as one watertight solid. Physical fit and tape
strength depend on printer calibration, filament and adhesive.
