# LM1 enclosure changelog

## 0.5.2 test prototype - 2026-09-17

- Replaced the 28-degree test camera bracket with a 15-degree downward bracket.
- Preserved the v0.5.1 mast interface, camera slots and print orientation for a direct swap.
- Added versioned STL output and mesh validation for the new bracket.

## 0.5.0 test prototype - 2026-09-06

- Added a separate three-piece open Pi tray, camera mast and 13-degree bracket.
- Reduced the test footprint to 140 x 130 mm, with accessible ports and cooling.
- Validated exported STL closure, positive volume and single-solid connectivity.
- Kept the full enclosure revisions available alongside the bench prototype.

## 0.4.0 - 2026-09-04

- Replaced the flat top display with a separate angled OLED pod.
- Tilted the OLED 35 degrees above horizontal for visibility at address.
- Added a rotating cable collar and dual locking arcs for 35 degrees of yaw
  adjustment toward either a left- or right-handed player.
- Split the display assembly into a top lid, swivel base, tilted cradle and
  replaceable OLED retainer.

## 0.3.0 - 2026-09-04

- Reduced the enclosure body from 210 mm to 170 mm wide.
- Moved the two LED modules below the camera and its wide-angle hood.
- Replaced the large one-piece chassis with a base, front panel, rear panel,
  two reversible side panels and four universal corner rails.
- Added recessed base holes for an optional steel ballast plate.
- Kept the camera, hood, window, OLED and LED adapters independently replaceable.
- Kept every individual printable part within 180 x 174 mm.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-09-04

- Added a printable Raspberry Pi 5 chassis with standard mounting bosses.
- Fixed the camera optical centre at 150 mm and the camera plate at 13 degrees downward.
- Added a removable wide-angle sun hood and replaceable window retainer.
- Added a removable top with a provisional 2.79-inch OLED opening and clamp.
- Added dual LED apertures, a 40 mm rear fan pattern and cross-flow vents.
- Exported separate watertight STL parts and an assembly reference.
